SPFClock

Sunscreen wears off on a schedule, and "I put some on this morning" is not a schedule. SPFClock turns your skin type, SPF and activity into a real reapply countdown you can actually follow.

Your real protection window

Skin type x SPF, capped at the two-hour dermatologist rule, cut to 40 minutes when you swim and 80 when you sweat.

Live reapply countdown

Log an application, get a live countdown and the exact time to reapply. The last 10 minutes turn into a warning.

Day in the sun, tallied

Every application is logged, so you can see how many protected hours the day actually added up to.

Private by design

No account, no location tracking. Everything stays in your browser's local storage.
